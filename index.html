On the road

The internet travels by road.

Lectures are recorded at the Ilm Centre, encrypted card by card and loaded into the van. The sync box on its roof carries new lessons out and brings homework back.

The village

Pay like a mobile recharge.

Cards and scratch vouchers reach the local mobile shop. Families text the voucher number and the unlock code comes back by SMS, which works on plain 2G. No bank account, no wallet, no data.

Ek chai se kam, roz ka ilm.

Government school

Homework up, new lessons down.

When the van parks, its sync box collects homework and quiz results from every phone nearby and sends back new lessons and grades. The government school becomes the Sunday exam centre, and mothers learn how to follow their children's progress.

After sunset

Lamp on. Wi-Fi off. Learning on.

Children study from the country's best teachers on a parent's phone, with no signal at all. Stuck on a question? Ustad AI searches the lectures on the phone and jumps to the exact moment that explains it.

Why we exist

With government
